Flight-sim environment systems

ProblemUnreal's defaults give one uniform cloud layer, water and grass that ignore aircraft, and runway lights that vanish at approach range. Training sims need all three to behave.

  • Wedge-shaped volumetric cloud: modified the engine cloud material with a BoxMask-3D boundary, with position, extent, rotation, slope, density and erosion all set at runtime through a Material Parameter Collection
  • Rotor wash in Niagara that only spawns below 3000 UU and gets stronger as the helicopter descends
  • The same downwash value drives circular ripples on FluidFlux water (6 force sources orbiting the aircraft) and radial grass bending
  • Runway lights readable at ~20 km without blowing out up close (distance-scaled sprites), placed with PCG at 60 m spacing, plus a 4-unit PAPI with correct white/red indication

About

Gameplay feel over fancy visuals

Started in Unity. Moved to Unreal to build the hard stuff.

I started with Unity and C#: a year of building prototypes for movement, physics, health and enemy AI, then publishing my own puzzle game on Google Play. That taught me to finish things, not just start them. In 2026 I moved to Unreal Engine 5 and spent six months at Akiyam Solutions building effects and environment systems for aviation and defence training sims.

The work I enjoy most sits where gameplay and rendering meet.
